Persona 1: Sam

Sam the "Book-worm"

As a book-lover, I want to know what books my close friends circle like and dislikes because I am curious and share similar interests. (add)

As a fan of books, I want to get recommendations for TV shows based on books, so I can get my friends who don’t read to enjoy the same series. (add)

As a bookworm, I want to keep track of all the books I've read and get recommendations for similar books based on my reading history and other users'.

As a college student, I want to be able to share my profile and recommendations, so that I can make new friends.

As someone who is always busy I don’t have a lot of time to go down recommendation rabbit holes, I want a platform that efficiently summarizes my likes so that I am able to find the next thing that I love without taking all my time.

As an “occasional” reader, I want to read more, but maybe I just haven’t found an easy experience for finding new books. Recommendations from friends sometimes might not be the best, and my co-workers don’t really know my taste - which has led me to say “I like to read” but really my books sit collecting dust.
